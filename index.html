Hi, I’m Vinnie! Welcome to my slice of the internet. Born and rooted in New Jersey with an electric daily love affair with New York City, I created @bewithvinnie to document unfiltered life at the intersection of beauty, hustle, culinary cravings, and culture.

Whether I'm hunting down the silkiest cacio e pepe in the Village, sharing high-low style hacks that survive subway commutes, hosting South Asian cultural gatherings, or decompressing with candid life chats on my podcast mic, this space is dedicated to celebrating the rich, joyful chaos of everyday living.
